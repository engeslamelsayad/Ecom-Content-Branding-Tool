# Hook Archetypes Reference — 35+ Types

## What Makes a Hook Work

A hook has ONE job: stop the scroll and create an irresistible compulsion to keep consuming.
A hook is NOT an introduction. It drops the reader/viewer mid-scene — into tension, curiosity, or emotion — with zero warm-up.

**The 3-Second Rule (Video):** If it doesn't hook in 3 seconds, it's dead. No exceptions.
**The 2-Line Rule (Copy):** If the first 2 lines don't create a reason to read line 3, it fails.

**Hook Selection Logic:**
- Awareness Stage 1–2 → Disruption hooks (Pattern Interrupt, Pain, Fear Forward)
- Awareness Stage 3 → Differentiation hooks (Myth-Busting, Comparison, Mechanism Reveal)
- Awareness Stage 4–5 → Validation hooks (Social Proof, Result, Urgency)
- All stages → Story and Curiosity hooks always work

---

## CATEGORY 1: Pain & Emotion Hooks

### 1. Pain-Trigger Hook (Mid-Scene Immersion)
Drop the viewer/reader directly into a specific, vivid pain moment.

**Structure:** [Specific scenario] + [Physical/emotional detail] → "Sound familiar?"

**Examples:**
- "محدش صحيح الساعة 2 الصبح عشان ظهره بيوجعه — بس أنا كنت بعمل كده كل يوم."
- "You've reorganized that drawer 4 times this month. It still looks like chaos."
- "لما بتقف ورا الكاشير وتكتشف إن حسابك مش كافي — التاني ده."

**Visual direction:** Show someone mid-experience-of-pain (not smiling at the problem — actually feeling it)

---

### 2. Fear-Forward Hook
Shows the painful future consequence of not acting. Future-paces the cost of inaction.

**Structure:** "By [timeframe], [specific negative outcome] if [nothing changes]."

**Examples:**
- "If you're still doing [X] in 2025, here's what it's costing you silently."
- "The average [avatar] loses $[X] per year by not solving this. Are you one of them?"
- "كل يوم بيفوت من غير ما تحل [المشكلة]، النتيجة بتبقى أصعب."

---

### 3. Frustration & Exhaustion Hook
Validates accumulated, ongoing frustration. "You've tried everything and nothing worked."

**Structure:** "Tried [X]. Tried [Y]. Tried [Z]. Nothing worked. Here's why."

**Examples:**
- "لو جربت كل حاجة وما شفتيش نتيجة، مش إنتي المشكلة."
- "Every diet you've tried worked for 2 weeks. Then stopped. There's a specific reason for that."

---

### 4. Embarrassment / Vulnerability Hook
Opens with a vulnerable, specific, relatable moment of embarrassment or failure.

**Examples:**
- "أول ما وقفت قدام العميل وعقلي وقف معايا — قررت أبدأ أتعلم."
- "I used to hide my [product category problem] from everyone. Wear long sleeves in summer. Avoid mirrors."

**Use carefully:** Must feel authentic, not manufactured. Generic embarrassment = no impact.

---

### 5. The "Before I Knew" Hook
Creates a clear "before/after" knowledge gap. Reader feels they're about to learn something that changes everything.

**Examples:**
- "قبل ما أعرف الطريقة دي، كنت بضيع [وقت / فلوس / مجهود]."
- "Before I discovered this, I was making this mistake every single day."

---

## CATEGORY 2: Curiosity & Open Loop Hooks

### 6. The Curiosity Gap Hook
Creates an information void the brain desperately wants to fill.

**Structure:** Name a result or revelation, withhold the HOW.

**Examples:**
- "السبب الحقيقي اللي خلى [outcome] — مش اللي بتفكر فيه."
- "The thing 97% of [avatars] don't know about [category] — and it's costing them."
- "هتقولولي مجنون — بس الطريقة دي وفرت عليا [X] كل شهر."

---

### 7. Controversial Opinion Hook
States a strong, polarizing opinion that the target avatar will either strongly agree or strongly disagree with.

**Structure:** "Unpopular opinion: [statement that challenges a widely held belief in your niche]."

**Examples:**
- "Unpopular opinion: Most [category] products are designed to keep you buying more, not to solve your problem."
- "رأي مش هيعجب كتير: ال [category] الغالي مش دايما أحسن."

**Power:** Strong agree = immediate bond. Strong disagree = engaged argument. Both drive engagement.

---

### 8. "What Nobody Tells You" Hook
Frames the hook as insider information being revealed for the first time.

**Examples:**
- "What nobody in the [industry] will tell you about [topic]."
- "اللي مش بيقولهولك محدش عن [category]."
- "The [industry] doesn't want you to know this — but [revelation]."

---

### 9. The Open Loop / Cliff-hanger Hook
Begins a story or revelation then pauses before completing it.

**Examples:**
- "كنت مش هاصدق اللي لقيته لما اشتريت ده — هقولك بعد شوية."
- "I almost returned it after day 1. What happened on day 3 changed everything."
- "It started with a bad review. Then things got interesting."

---

### 10. The "Real Reason" Revelation Hook
Reframes a common problem, revealing the true underlying cause.

**Structure:** "The reason [common symptom] is actually [unexpected root cause] — not [what they thought]."

**Examples:**
- "السبب الحقيقي إنك مش بتنام كويس مش الإجهاد — ده [mechanism]."
- "The real reason your skin keeps breaking out isn't diet or stress — it's this."

---

## CATEGORY 3: Social Proof & Authority Hooks

### 11. Hard Result / Transformation Hook
Opens with a specific, quantified, credible result. No warm-up — straight to the outcome.

**Structure:** "[Avatar] + [Specific result] + [Timeframe] — here's exactly how."

**Examples:**
- "فاطمة خست 12 كيلو في 60 يوم من غير ما تدخل جيم."
- "From $0 to $43,000 in 90 days — using a system most people overlook."

**Critical rule:** The result must be hyper-specific and believable (not too good, not too vague).

---

### 12. Verified Buyer Volume Hook
Leads with a credibility number that triggers social proof.

**Examples:**
- "+94,000 customer reviews can't be wrong about this."
- "فوق ٥٠ ألف عميل جربوه — والنتيجة واحدة دايما."
- "When 1,200 verified buyers say the same thing, it's worth paying attention."

---

### 13. Celebrity / Expert Reference Hook
Borrows authority from a recognizable figure (must be verified/legal).

**Examples:**
- "The same method [credentialed expert] uses with their own clients."
- "Why [relevant authority] switched from [old way] to this."

---

### 14. Media / Recognition Hook
Opens with a media feature or award — borrowed credibility.

**Examples:**
- "As seen in Forbes, Business Insider, and 3 industry publications."
- "اتكلم عنه في [media outlet] — وبعدها اتغير كل حاجة."

---

## CATEGORY 4: Disruption & Pattern Interrupt Hooks

### 15. The Contradiction Hook
States something that sounds impossible or counter-intuitive, forcing the brain to reconcile the paradox.

**Examples:**
- "The more you try to fix [problem], the worse it gets. Here's why."
- "كل ما اشتريت أكتر، ما وفرتش أكتر."
- "Rest days build more muscle than training days. Science explains."

---

### 16. The "Stop Doing X" Hook
Challenges a deeply ingrained behavior with authority.

**Structure:** "Stop [common behavior everyone does]. Here's what to do instead."

**Examples:**
- "Stop washing your face twice a day. Your skin barrier is begging you."
- "وقف تشرب [شيء شائع] مع الأكل — وشوف الفرق."

---

### 17. The Shocking Statistic Hook
Opens with a surprising, specific, real number that reframes the scale of the problem.

**Examples:**
- "67% of people with [condition] are using the wrong solution — and making it worse."
- "The average [avatar] wastes [X hours / $ amount] per year on [ineffective approach]."
- "٨٤٪ من اللي جربوا [category] وفشلوا كانوا بيغلطوا في حاجة واحدة بس."

---

### 18. The True/False Challenge Hook
Poses an interactive question that forces the viewer to form an opinion.

**Examples:**
- "True or false: [Common misconception in your niche]? (Most people get this wrong.)"
- "صح ولا غلط: [common belief]؟ — الجواب ممكن يفاجئك."

---

### 19. The Extreme Close-Up / Zoom Hook (Video)
A macro shot of a specific, unexpected detail — no context, just the close-up — forces curiosity.

**Visual direction:** Extreme macro of product texture, ingredient, mechanism component.
No audio needed in first 2 frames. Let curiosity build, THEN reveal what it is.

---

### 20. The Blank Screen / Text-Only Hook
Single word or short phrase on a clean background. Maximum contrast with busy visual feeds.

**Examples:**
- Black screen. White text: "درك." [pause 1.5s] "خليك."
- White screen. One word: "ENOUGH." [then story begins]

---

## CATEGORY 5: Platform-Native & Format Hooks

### 21. UI Simulation Hook — AirDrop / Notification
Mimics a native phone notification to trigger automatic interaction reflex.

**Types:**
- AirDrop: "Ahmed wants to share a file: 'How to fix [pain point].pdf'" → Accept / Decline
- iMessage notification: "[Name]: have you tried [product]? my [result] completely changed"
- Missed call + voicemail text transcript = testimonial
- Notes app entry: "Day 3 update — I can't believe..."

**Production note:** Must use exact iOS/Android UI specs. Wrong font or color breaks immersion.

---

### 22. Mock SERP / Google Search Hook
Frames the ad as a native Google search results page. Borrows search-intent credibility.

**Visual:** Google search bar with: "best [product category] for [pain]"
**Results:** Your product appears as the top organic result with benefit-led meta description.

---

### 23. Reddit / Forum Thread Hook
Mimics a forum discussion. Someone asks "Does [product] actually work?" — multiple replies give
social proof in a native, authentic format.

**Upvote visual:** Highest upvoted answer = your key selling point.

---

### 24. SMS / DM Thread Hook
Conversation format between two friends discussing the product. Captures word-of-mouth credibility.

**Include:** A hesitation moment + overcome moment for authenticity.

---

### 25. Scrolling TikTok / Comment Section Hook
Shows a TikTok video with a comment section visible. Comments are social proof statements.
Creator responds to a top comment with a product reveal.

---

## CATEGORY 6: Story & Identity Hooks

### 26. The Confession Hook
Personal revelation that creates immediate intimacy and trust.

**Structure:** "I used to [embarrassing/limiting belief or behavior]. Then I discovered [revelation]."

**Examples:**
- "كنت فاكر إن [wrong belief]. غلطت."
- "I used to think expensive meant effective. I was spending $200/month on the wrong thing."

---

### 27. The "I Almost Quit" Hook
Begins with a near-failure moment that creates narrative tension.

**Examples:**
- "I was 3 days away from shutting everything down. Then this happened."
- "كنت بقالي أسبوع مش شايف نتيجة. يوم ٨ غير كل حاجة."

---

### 28. The Stranger's Story Hook
Opens with overhearing or observing someone else's transformation. Third-person credibility.

**Examples:**
- "The woman in front of me at checkout had [result]. I asked her what she was using."
- "ست جنبي في الأتوبيس قالت جملة واحدة غيرت طريقة تفكيري في [topic]."

---

### 29. The Identity Challenge Hook
Directly challenges who the reader believes they are, or who they want to be.

**Examples:**
- "هل إنت فعلاً [identity claim the avatar wants]?"
- "If you actually care about [value they hold], why are you still doing [contradictory behavior]?"

---

### 30. The Founder / Origin Story Hook
Explains why the product was created — built from personal pain.

**Examples:**
- "I built this because I couldn't find anything that actually worked for [specific situation]."
- "بنيت [brand] لأني عشت [pain] لمدة [X سنوات] ومحدش كان عنده الحل الصح."

---

## CATEGORY 7: Offer & Urgency Hooks

### 31. The Transparent Offer Hook
Opens with the offer itself — no build-up, just the value stacked immediately.

**Best for:** Most Aware audiences who just need the deal.

**Examples:**
- "Free [X] with every order — but only this week."
- "اليوم بس: اشتري [X] وجيب [Y] مجاناً."

---

### 32. The "Last Time" Scarcity Hook
Creates finality and irreversibility.

**Examples:**
- "This is the last time we'll offer [X] at this price."
- "آخر مرة هنعمل العرض ده بسعره ده."

**Rule:** Only use if genuinely true. Fake scarcity destroys long-term trust permanently.

---

### 33. The Price Reframe Hook
Reframes the cost of the product as absurdly small relative to the alternative.

**Examples:**
- "Less than $1 per day to never deal with [pain] again."
- "بأقل من كوباية قهوة يومياً — بتحل [مشكلة كبيرة]."

---

### 34. The Comparison Hook (Side-by-Side Open)
Opens by showing the contrast — what life/results look like with vs. without the product.

**Visual:** Split screen. Left = without. Right = with.
**Copy:** "Same [time period]. Completely different [result]."

---

### 35. The "What If" Hook
Opens a possibility space — plants an aspirational vision before making any claim.

**Examples:**
- "What if you could [desired outcome] without [biggest obstacle]?"
- "إيه لو قدرت [outcome] من غير ما [sacrifice]?"

---

## Hook Combinations for Maximum Impact (Video)

For video ads, pair a **visual hook** with an **audio hook** from different categories:

| Visual Hook | Audio Hook | Effect |
|-------------|-----------|--------|
| Extreme Close-Up (19) | Curiosity Gap (6) | "What IS that?" → "What does it do?" |
| UI Simulation (21) | Open Loop (9) | Native reflex → narrative pull |
| Blank Screen (20) | Pain-Trigger (1) | Contrast disruption → emotional resonance |
| Split-screen Before/After | Hard Result (11) | Visual proof → specific credibility |
| Founder talking direct-to-camera | Confession (26) | Intimacy → trust → revelation |
